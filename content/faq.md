# Frequently Asked Questions

Thinking about bringing trivia to your bar, pub, or private event? Here are answers to the questions we hear most often.

---

<details>
<summary>What exactly does Binge Thinkers provide?</summary>
<p>We bring a <strong>professional host</strong>, all the <strong>questions and rounds</strong>, a <strong>laptop</strong>, and <strong>answer sheets</strong>. Your host runs the game and keeps the room lively. You bring the crowd and, ideally, a screen.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the venue need to provide?</summary>
<p>We need a <strong>TV or projector</strong> with HDMI, space for teams, and room for your crowd. We will also help you choose simple <strong>prizes</strong>, such as a bar tab, gift card, merch, or bragging rights. Food and drinks stay in your hands.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does a trivia night run?</summary>
<p>A standard night lasts about <strong>two hours</strong>, including several rounds, a break, and a final. Need a different length? We can adjust.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How does booking work?</summary>
<p>Use our <a href="/contact">contact form</a> or call us. Share your date, location, and crowd size. We'll confirm availability within <strong>24 hours</strong> and send a quote.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do we get to pick our host?</summary>
<p>You don't need to choose. <strong>We assign a host</strong> who fits the night. If you have worked with us before and have a favourite, let us know.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What kind of trivia do you run?</summary>
<p>We write and update our questions in-house. Expect <strong>pop culture, sports, history, music, and excellent bar arguments</strong>. It is competitive enough to matter and relaxed enough for everyone.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How is this different from running our own trivia?</summary>
<p>We've done this for <strong>over 15 years</strong> in dozens of venues. You get a proven format, fresh questions, and a host whose only job is giving your crowd a great night.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can we book a one-off night, or do you do recurring trivia?</summary>
<p>Both. We run <strong>one-off private events</strong> and <strong>weekly or monthly nights</strong> for bars and pubs. We handle the game. You focus on your guests.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you do corporate or private events?</summary>
<p>Yes. Team events, holiday parties, launches, and celebrations all work. We can add themes, inside jokes, or custom questions. Tell us your idea on the <a href="/contact">contact form</a>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How many people can play?</summary>
<p>Trivia works best in <strong>teams of 3 to 6</strong>. We have hosted crowds from 20 to more than 200. Tell us your headcount and we'll shape the game around it.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What about prizes?</summary>
<p><strong>You and the host coordinate prizes.</strong> A bar tab, gift card, round of drinks, or pure bragging rights all work. We will help you pick what suits the crowd.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does it cost?</summary>
<p>Price depends on the crowd, date, frequency, and any custom work. <strong><a href="/contact">Ask us for a quote</a></strong> and we'll keep it clear and practical.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How far in advance should we book?</summary>
<p>A few weeks is usually enough for a <strong>one-off event</strong>. Book earlier for recurring nights or popular dates. Once you have a date in mind, ask us.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What areas do you serve?</summary>
<p>We're based in <strong>Winnipeg</strong>. Planning something nearby or unusual? Ask us. We like interesting plans.</p>
</details>
