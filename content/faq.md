# Frequently Asked Questions

Thinking about bringing trivia to your bar, pub, or private event? Here are answers to the questions we hear most often.

---

## What exactly does Binge Thinkers provide?

We bring a **professional host**, all the **questions and rounds**, a **laptop**, and **answer sheets**. Your host runs the game and keeps the room lively. You bring the crowd and, ideally, a screen.

## What does the venue need to provide?

We need a **TV or projector** with HDMI, space for teams, and room for your crowd. We will also help you choose simple **prizes**, such as a bar tab, gift card, merch, or bragging rights. Food and drinks stay in your hands.

## How long does a trivia night run?

A standard night lasts about **two hours**, including several rounds, a break, and a final. Need a different length? We can adjust.

## How does booking work?

Use our [contact form](/contact) or call us. Share your date, location, and crowd size. We'll confirm availability within **24 hours** and send a quote.

## Do we get to pick our host?

You don't need to choose. **We assign a host** who fits the night. If you have worked with us before and have a favourite, let us know.

## What kind of trivia do you run?

We write and update our questions in-house. Expect **pop culture, sports, history, music, and excellent bar arguments**. It is competitive enough to matter and relaxed enough for everyone.

## How is this different from running our own trivia?

We've done this for **over 15 years** in dozens of venues. You get a proven format, fresh questions, and a host whose only job is giving your crowd a great night.

## Can we book a one-off night, or do you do recurring trivia?

Both. We run **one-off private events** and **weekly or monthly nights** for bars and pubs. We handle the game. You focus on your guests.

## Do you do corporate or private events?

Yes. Team events, holiday parties, launches, and celebrations all work. We can add themes, inside jokes, or custom questions. Tell us your idea on the [contact form](/contact).

## How many people can play?

Trivia works best in **teams of 3 to 6**. We have hosted crowds from 20 to more than 200. Tell us your headcount and we'll shape the game around it.

## What about prizes?

**You and the host coordinate prizes.** A bar tab, gift card, round of drinks, or pure bragging rights all work. We will help you pick what suits the crowd.

## How much does it cost?

Price depends on the crowd, date, frequency, and any custom work. **[Ask us for a quote](/contact)** and we'll keep it clear and practical.

## How far in advance should we book?

A few weeks is usually enough for a **one-off event**. Book earlier for recurring nights or popular dates. Once you have a date in mind, ask us.

## What areas do you serve?

We're based in **Winnipeg**. Planning something nearby or unusual? Ask us. We like interesting plans.
