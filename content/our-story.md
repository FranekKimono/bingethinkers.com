# Our Story

Binge Thinkers trivia nights began 15 years ago in Seoul, South Korea. It instantly became a hit among the English speaking expat community.

<img src="/jeff.sinclair-black-wide.jpg" class="story-wide" alt="Jeff Sinclair, stand-up comedian and curator of Binge Thinkers trivia content" />

From there it grew into a series of nights at venues in various cities all over the peninsula. It was listed as the best trivia night in the country in Groove Magazine, the most prominent English publication on the peninsula.

## Building Something Real

We built custom rounds, league nights, and corporate events across South Korea. The rooms kept filling up. Our hosts became familiar faces. The questions got sharper. The nights got louder.

## A New Chapter in Winnipeg

Now Winnipeg gets the best of what we learned in Seoul: clever questions, experienced hosts, creative categories, and a multimedia extravaganza people want to repeat.
